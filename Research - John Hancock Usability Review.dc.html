John Hancock Investments — Usability Review

A fast, expert-led review rather than a full study — focused on practical usability issues, not just the obvious ones. I assessed the John Hancock Investments application against six core UX parameters.

Review Parameters
Information Design
Interaction Design
Visual Design
Navigation System
Search Effectiveness
Error Tolerance & Feedback
Purpose of Evaluation

The purpose was to evaluate John Hancock Investments' features against heuristic principles to improve the UX of the JH webpage. Negative findings were ranked 1–5 (minor to severe usability problem); positive findings weren't ranked. Every negative finding carries a recommendation.

POSITIVE FINDINGS

Aesthetic & Minimalist Design — Clean design

The home page is busy, but there's visual hierarchy of information and a clear call to action.

Flexibility & Efficiency of Use — Tailored for frequent actions

Autofill behavior is supported, making form filling faster and easier.

Visibility of System Status — Users stay informed

The screen clearly states what data is available right in the header.

NEGATIVE FINDINGS

Consistency & Standards

MILD

Issue: The right-side menu acts as the main nav, but shows selected/deselected inconsistently depending on which menu you're on.

Recommendation: Keep the main menu's color and behavior consistent across screens.

Error Prevention

MILD

Issue: Errors surface only after submission — users can type letters into numeric date fields, and multi-select isn't available for fund search.

Recommendation: Prevent invalid input at entry time, enforce a clear date format, and add multi-select to fund search.

Recognition Rather Than Recall — Icons

MODERATE

Issue: Compare, share, add, remove and view-documents actions are all icon-only — users have to remember what each one means.

Recommendation: Bridge the visual elements with the actions users actually need to take — labels or tooltips alongside icons.

Recognition Rather Than Recall — Jargon

MODERATE

Issue: Fund detail pages carry a high memory load — terms like Alpha, Beta, and Sharpe ratio are shown with no explanation.

Recommendation: Add tooltips and explanations for jargon; make tutorial/support options clearly visible in the main menu.

Aesthetic & Minimalist Design

MODERATE

Issue: In the dashboard, user attention is dominated by Viewpoints, which captures roughly 80% of the real estate.

Recommendation: Let users set their own preference order for Portfolio, Investment, Add Fund, Viewpoints and other modules.
